# Studio campaign library: sources and measurement

Reviewed October 27, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). The collection contains **20 specific campaign studies**, **22 bundled creative previews** and **three click-to-play official films**. Each study explains the execution, a creative interpretation and a practical adaptation brief. This is not a ranking of marketing effectiveness.

Most campaigns ran in 2024-2025. Heinz, Orange, Pop-Tarts, Specsavers and Barbie retain distinct 2023 lessons. Ramp is a 2026 example. Wispr's media kit does not publish the film's release date; it is an undated creative reference. A later article or award-entry date does not change the campaign year.

## Results ledger

Figures retain the reporting party, unit, window and limitations. Reported results include participation and service actions, not only sales. Reach includes attention and search measures. Wispr and Ramp have no verified campaign-level performance. Strategic explanations are our interpretation, including when public results are reported.

Three verified films provide varied film structures; unplayed webpage links are not labeled as videos. Films use click-to-load `youtube-nocookie.com` embeds, public-availability checks and player error handling. Playback was checked on this Mac and connection, not every region or future date. Films are not downloaded or bundled. If a publisher restricts playback later, the study, local poster, original link and creation action remain available.

Third-party previews are for study, with credits and originals, not reusable assets for a founder's ad. No generated imitation is presented as original work. Local copies render on a fresh offline workspace. If a bundle is absent, bounded downloads, binary/dimension validation and the last-good cache remain available. Revisioned URLs avoid retaining replaced creative in a previous release's browser cache.

## Product organization and writing contract

- **Create:** format, occasion and optional brief. Recommendations match audience, format and supported techniques; they do not rank incomparable outcome metrics. Custom inspiration remains optional.
- **Campaign library:** search/filter 20 studies by audience, job and outcomes. Deep links open The campaign, Why it worked (Creative logic for unmeasured references), and Make it yours. Context, fit, failure conditions and sources remain accessible with a persistent creation action.
- **Your work:** saved pieces retain the selected approach and supplied examples, including Write more. Brief/example input survives browsing and browser history.
- **Brand & material:** profile, voice, claims, stories and images remain in a drawer.

Old software launches, Cider clips and incomplete records are excluded from active discovery and recommendations. Archived IDs remain resolvable for previously saved work. The unused Supabase cover is removed.

Campaigns shape creative direction, not product evidence. Outcome numbers and media are omitted from the writer's campaign context. Only the founder's own receipts substantiate claims. Custom examples are untrusted reference text. Existing lint, revision, blind-reader review, missing-material checks and manual approval remain. These checks do not establish that a generated campaign improves conversion.

Regression coverage includes curation, archival compatibility, asset provenance, first-launch offline previews, rejected media, last-good caching, search/filtering, deep links, keyboard tabs, history, scrolling, responsive layouts, creation continuity and saved provenance. Live checks complement those fixtures; mocked browser media alone do not prove publisher availability.
